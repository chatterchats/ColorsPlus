## [1.0.0]

### Added

- First public release.
- A Custom Color picker (color box, hue slider and hex input, with a live
  preview) under supported color choices.
- Main-menu character creator and barracks character editor: clothing and
  armor, hair, skin (including species skin tones such as Zabrak), makeup,
  tattoos, horns and scars.
- Armory: the Paint Color of blasters, previewed while you change Location
  and Paint Finish.
- Armor wearing a color the palette no longer offers (for example from an
  unlocker mod) can be changed.
